ASH Draw Studio is a free drawing viewer and editor for DXF and DWG files, for Windows. It works in a way that will feel familiar if you use AutoCAD: a command line with the usual commands and aliases, object snaps, layers, layouts and viewports.

## Free and open
The program is released under the MIT licence by ASH Technical & Project Management Services (ASH PMCS). Download it from [ashdesignsuite.com](https://ashdesignsuite.com); the company site is [ashpmcs.com](https://ashpmcs.com). **Help ▸ About** shows the version and the licence notices.

## DXF and DWG
- **DXF** files are read and written directly.
- **DWG** files are converted when you open them. The program includes the free LibreDWG converter. You can also install the free **ODA File Converter** from the Open Design Alliance: it opens drawings the built-in converter cannot read and adds saving to DWG (2000 to 2018). Everything else works without it. See [DWG converter](#topic:dwg-converter).

## Works offline
The program does not need an internet connection. Your drawings stay on your computer.
