# Display options

Most display settings are in the **View** menu. The status bar buttons switch the same settings ([show me](#showme:status:grid)).

## Grid

1. Press <kbd>F7</kbd>, click **GRID** in the status bar, or choose **View ▸ Grid**.
2. For spacing, type `GRID` and choose an option:

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ON / OFF | Show or hide the grid |
| Spacing | Distance between grid lines (on a layout, in paper millimetres) |
| Major | Grid lines per major line, 1 to 100 |
| Style | Lines or Dots |

> **Note:** <kbd>F9</kbd> (**Snap to grid**) is separate. It snaps the pointer to the grid; `SNAP` holds its settings.

## Lineweights and transparency

- **LWT** shows lineweights at their real thickness ([show me](#showme:status:lineweights)). **View ▸ Show lineweights** does the same.
- **TPY** shows transparency ([show me](#showme:status:transparency)).

Switching them off does not change the drawing, only how it is drawn.

## Colours

1. **View ▸ Model space background: dark** switches the model space between a dark and a light background. It applies whichever theme is on.
2. **View ▸ Dark theme** darkens the menus and panels. The **DARK** button at the right of the status bar does the same ([show me](#showme:el:theme-btn)).

## Markups

**View ▸ Show markups** hides or shows review markups without deleting them. See [markup review](#topic:markup-review).

## LTSCALE

If dashed lines look too tight or too loose:

1. Type `LTSCALE` (`LTS`) and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
2. Enter a number greater than 0. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> alone to keep the current value.

> **Tip:** Use `REGEN` if the display does not update after a change. See [zoom and pan](#topic:navigate-zoom-pan) and [drafting aids](#topic:drafting-aids).
