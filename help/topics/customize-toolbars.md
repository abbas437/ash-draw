# Customize the toolbars and panels

You can move the tool panel, change its layout, hide tools you never use and build your own Quick Access row. Everything is under **View ▸ Toolbars**.

## Place and shape the tool panel

1. Open **View ▸ Toolbars** ([show me](#showme:el:tools)).
2. Choose **Tool panel: Left**, **Right**, **Top** or **Hidden**.
3. Choose a layout:

| Layout | What you get |
|---|---|
| Full | Every group with its tool buttons |
| Compact rail | One button per group; click it to open a flyout of that group's tools |
| Floating | The panel in a small window you drag by its title strip |
| Floating compact | The compact rail's group buttons in a floating window |

4. Switch **Tool labels** on to show names beside the icons, and **Tool group colours** to colour-code the groups.
5. **Tools: Compact** folds every group; **Tools: Expanded** opens them all again. You can also click a group header to fold just that group.

> **Tip:** Hidden does not remove the tools. Their command aliases, such as `LINE` (`L`), still work.

## Customize tools

1. Choose **View ▸ Toolbars ▸ Customize tools…**
2. On the **Tool panel** tab, untick a tool to hide its button, use the arrows to reorder tools or groups, or pick another group from the list beside a tool. **All** and **None** show or hide a whole group.
3. On the **Quick Access** tab, pick a command and choose **Add**, or **Add separator**. Use the arrows to reorder and **Remove** to take an item out.
4. Click **OK** to apply, or **Reset to default** to start again.

> **Note:** Hidden tools still run from the command line and from the menus. Only the button goes.

## The Quick Access row

**View ▸ Toolbars ▸ Quick Access row** shows or hides the row under the menu bar ([show me](#showme:el:qat)).

## Right-click shortcuts

Right-click a tool button to hide it or add it to Quick Access. Right-click a Quick Access button to remove it. The same menu ends with all the **View ▸ Toolbars** items. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F10</kbd> opens it from the keyboard.

## The side panel

The side panel holds Layers, Markups, Symbols and Properties ([show me](#showme:panel:side)). Use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>3</kbd> to switch between Expanded and Collapsed, or choose **View ▸ Panels ▸ Side panel: Expanded / Collapsed / Hidden**. Collapsed leaves a narrow rail of icons; click one to open that section over the drawing. Click a section header to fold it.
